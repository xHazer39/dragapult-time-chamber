# Matchup: Dragapult mirror

Priority: **highest**. Dragapult was 29.37% of Worlds 2026 day 2 (42 of 143 decks, Limitless statistics) and Hedrick
played it in at least four of his fifteen rounds.

## Strategic objective
Win the early Budew item-lock exchange, build a full board while they cannot play Items, and only then commit an
attacker. [claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_8, _9, _2]

## Prize map
No source gives explicit prize arithmetic for the mirror. What is stated: going in too early lets them answer with
Crushing Hammer + Unfair Stamp and get far ahead. [claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_2] **Missing evidence.**

## Liabilities
- Attacking first with an incomplete board. [claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_9]
- Letting your own Budew die while theirs lives: they get Items back and you do not. [claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_8]
- A single Munkidori + Darkness Energy answer can undo your attack: check it before committing. [claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_12]

## Resources to preserve
- Darkness Energy: the third copy exists to get Munkidori online early and to survive Crushing Hammer.
  [claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_7] Recover it *before* the third Hammer, not after. [claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_21]
- Ultra Ball matters less here (item lock, big hands); Hedrick cut to three partly for this reason.
  [claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_5]

## Disruption priorities
Crushing Hammer on the Darkness Energy that powers their Munkidori, so the damage you spread cannot be moved back.
[claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_3]

## Common decision families
setup, bench, attach, attack timing, damage_target.
