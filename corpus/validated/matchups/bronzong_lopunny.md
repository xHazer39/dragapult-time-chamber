# Matchup: Bronzong / Lopunny

Priority: **medium**. A deck built to counter Dragapult; Hedrick calls the matchup close when you know what you are
doing, and he had practised it specifically.

## Strategic objective
Bronzong stops your evolutions, so Fezandipiti ex becomes a primary attacker: Cruel Arrow does 100 to any of their
Pokémon and is not shut off by Bronzong. [claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_17]

## Target priority (from the one fully analysed turn)
Do **not** Knock Out the Bronzong just to free your evolutions: a single draw Supporter turns into Dusknoir plus
Lopunny and two Knock Outs. Knocking Out Dusclops denies the Dusknoir line and protects both your Drakloak and your
Fezandipiti ex; Knocking Out Buneary removes their attacker but leaves the Dusknoir line intact.
[claim_eeHvbC8bpXQ_15, _16]
