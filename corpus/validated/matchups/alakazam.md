# Matchup: Alakazam

Priority: **high**. 11.19% of Worlds 2026 day 2, and it was the World Championship final.

## Strategic objective
Use spread damage and hand disruption, the two things Alakazam struggles against. [claim_pokebeach_manley_1]
Hedrick considered the matchup favourable for his list, and was mainly afraid of a single Eri.

## Their counter-cards
- **Battle Cage**: protects their Bench from spread, unless you find one of your two Stadiums (Risky Ruins).
- **Eri**: stops Special Red Card or Unfair Stamp.
- **Genesect**: a more reliable answer to Unfair Stamp. [claim_pokebeach_manley_2]

## Liabilities
- Knocking Out the wrong Pokémon can trigger Flip the Script and hand them cards. [claim_OcmjWWv-daQ_31]
- Leaving them a turn to rebuild after a slow Itchy Pollen turn. [claim_OcmjWWv-daQ_30]

## Reads that matter
They skipped a Supporter, so they probably do not hold Hilda or Dawn; they searched Kadabra, so their hand is known
to contain it. Counting their five cards is what makes the aggressive turn-2 attack defensible.
